Our Mission

Fort Worth Premium Basketball League is a new competitive men's league launching from local Fort Worth rec centers. We target serious 25+ players while still giving skilled 18–24 year olds a real shot to earn a roster spot. Intermediate and advanced only — this is real runs, not pickup.

Every dollar collected goes directly back into the league — referees, jerseys, gym rental, insurance, basketballs, end-of-season trophies, and future growth. No one is getting rich off the players. This is about discipline, competition, and building something lasting that Fort Worth hoopers can be proud of.

Frequently Asked

What does the $125 season fee cover?
Refs for every regular-season and playoff game, league insurance, gym rental at Chisholm Trail and other Fort Worth venues, two reversible jerseys, game balls, scorekeeping, end-of-season trophies for champions and MVP, and a fund for league growth into year two. Twelve-week season.
What if I don't make the cut after tryouts?
If you aren't selected, your $10 tryout fee covers your participation in the open run and is non-refundable. You will not be charged the $125 season fee. We'll also connect you with other Fort Worth leagues we know of that might be a better skill fit.
Are refunds available?
The $10 tryout fee is non-refundable. The $125 season fee is refundable up to the first regular season game; after the first game, it is non-refundable. If the league cancels the season for any reason, season fees are fully refunded.
Who can play? Age limits?
You must be 18 or older to participate. Our focus is 25+ players, but skilled 18–24 year olds are welcome to try out and earn a roster spot. Skill level is intermediate or advanced — this is a competitive league.
When does the season start and end?
Tryouts: Saturday, June 27, 2026. Teams formed and announced the following week. Regular season tips off mid-July and runs 12 weeks through early October, followed by a single-elimination playoff bracket.
Where are games played?
Primary venue is Chisholm Trail Community Center. Some weeks games may rotate to other Fort Worth rec centers depending on court availability. Schedule will be posted here before the season starts.
How do I contact the league?
DM us on Instagram @fwpbl_official, text or call (817) 555-0123, or email contact@fwpbl.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Schedule format

Two game nights · four games a week

The league runs on a tight, predictable weekly rhythm so games are easy to plan around work and family. Every player gets at least one game every single week.

  • 2 game nights per week — same nights every week, locked once the season starts
  • 2 games per night — back-to-back at the same venue, so you can come watch the early game before yours
  • 4 games per week total across the whole league
  • At 8 teams: every team plays at least 1 game per week, guaranteed

Teams

Structure & expansion plan
Team formation

How rosters get built

Eight teams to start. Rosters are drafted from the tryout pool in the week after tryouts close. Captains are selected by league organizers based on tryout performance — you'll know who's wearing the C before opening night.

  • 8 teams · 5–7 players per roster
  • Balanced draft for competitive parity (no stacked teams)
  • Captains pick team names and reversible jersey colors
  • Final rosters announced here the week before opening night
Expansion plan

Demand keeps coming? We grow.

Eight teams is the baseline, not the ceiling. If we fill up and the waitlist keeps building, the plan is to open expansion before the season starts:

  • Waitlist opens automatically once the 40 tryout spots are claimed
  • Once 10+ players are on the waitlist, we open a second tryout
  • League grows to 10 teams — and beyond if demand keeps coming
  • Confirmed expansion announced here and on Instagram

Stats

Tracking the real numbers
What we track

Real stats, real recognition

This isn't rec ball with a guy keeping score on a napkin. Every game gets a real scoresheet. Every player gets credit for what they actually do on the floor. Leaders get featured here weekly.

  • Points · Rebounds · Assists · Steals · Blocks per game
  • Field goal % · 3-point % tracked all season
  • Team and individual W/L records
  • Weekly stat leaders posted here every Monday
How it works

Box scores within 24 hours

League scorekeepers track stats live during every game. Box scores published here within 24 hours of tip-off. The Stats tab updates throughout the season so every player can see exactly how they're stacking up across the league.

End-of-season awards

Earn your name on the board

  • Regular Season MVP — best overall player by combined stats and team impact
  • Scoring Champion — highest points per game
  • Defensive Player of the Year — top combined steals, blocks, defensive impact
  • All-League Team — top 5 selected by captains and stats
  • Playoff MVP — best player in the bracket

Award winners get their names archived here permanently — Season 1, Season 2, and every season after.
